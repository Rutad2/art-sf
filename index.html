About

What this is

Art SF helps residents and visitors discover artworks in San Francisco's Civic Art Collection. Search, filter by medium, neighborhood, or district, and save artworks you want to visit.

Where the data comes from

All information comes from the Civic Art Collection dataset on DataSF, the City and County of San Francisco's open data portal. Data as of April 17, 2024.

How the data was prepared

The full dataset has 1,038 records. This app shows 100 of them. Only artworks with a known neighborhood and map location were included, and artworks listed more than once appear once. Each artwork's medium was grouped into a simpler type for filtering.

Why the colored blocks?

The city's dataset doesn't include photos, so each artwork gets a color based on its type.
